Home Page

Ze Wang is an associate researcher in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Tsinghua University. His research focuses on intelligent planning and control for lithography stages, advanced CNC machine tools, and aerospace manufacturing equipment, together with hardware and software design for drive and control systems.

Research Interests

  • Intelligent manufacturing equipment
  • Precision motion control and planning
  • Embodied intelligence
  • Precision motion control of special-purpose electric motors
  • Trajectory planning for machine tools and robots
  • Intelligent adhesive bonding technologies and equipment

Education

  • September 2015–June 2020: Ph.D., Tsinghua University.
  • September 2011–July 2015: Bachelor’s degree, Tianjin University.

Appointments

  • January 2026–present: Associate Researcher, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Tsinghua University.
  • July 2022–December 2025: Assistant Researcher, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Tsinghua University.
  • June 2020–July 2022: Postdoctoral Researcher (Shuimu Tsinghua Scholar), Department of Mechanical Engineering, Tsinghua University.
