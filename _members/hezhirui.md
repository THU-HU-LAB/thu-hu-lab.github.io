Zhirui He received the B.E. degree in mechanical engineering and a minor B.S. degree in mathematics from the Department of Mechanical Engineering and the Department of Mathematical Sciences, respectively, Tsinghua University, Beijing, China, both in 2025. He is currently working toward the Ph.D. degree in mechanical engineering in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Tsinghua University. His research interests include robotic motion control and manipulation.

## Research Interests

- Robotic motion control
- Robotic manipulation
