Jinlai Xiang received the B.E. degree in mechanical engineering from Tsinghua University, Beijing, China, in 2024. His research focuses on precision metrology and intelligent modeling for advanced manufacturing, with particular interests in full-field measurement and reconstruction, machine-learning-based forward prediction, and inverse process design and optimization. His broader research interests include scientific machine learning, micro/nano manufacturing, and semiconductor manufacturing technologies.

## Research Interests

- Intelligent manufacturing
- Scientific machine learning
- Forward modeling and inverse process design
- Micro/nano and semiconductor manufacturing
