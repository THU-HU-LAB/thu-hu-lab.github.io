Chuxiong Hu is a tenured professor and Ph.D. supervisor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Tsinghua University. His research focuses on intelligent control of precision mechatronic systems for advanced equipment and robotics, with applications in integrated circuit manufacturing equipment, CNC systems, intelligent equipment, and robots.

## Research Interests

- Precision motion control and trajectory planning
- Intelligent control of robots
- Intelligent learning and prediction
- Precision and ultraprecision mechatronic motion systems

## Education

- 2005–2010: Ph.D. in Mechatronic Engineering, Zhejiang University.
- 2007–2008: Government-sponsored joint doctoral training, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Purdue University, USA.
- 2000–2005: B.Eng. in Mechatronic Engineering (five-year program), Zhejiang University.
