Jichuan Yu received the B.E. degree in mechanical engineering in 2021 from the Department of Mechanical Engineering, Tsinghua University, Beijing, China, where he is currently working toward the Ph.D. degree in mechanical engineering. His research interests include robotics, robot operating systems, vision-based tactile sensing, and robotic motion planning and control. He was the recipient of the Best Paper Award in Advanced Robotics at the 2024 IEEE International Conference on Advanced Robotics and Mechatronics.

## Research Interests

- Robotics and control
- Motion planning
- Robotic manipulation learning
