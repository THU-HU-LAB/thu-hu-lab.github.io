Zhao Jin (S’24) received the B.E. degree in mechanical engineering and a minor in computer science from the Department of Mechanical Engineering and the Department of Computer Science and Technology, respectively, Tsinghua University, Beijing, China, both in 2024. He is currently working toward the Ph.D. degree in mechanical engineering with the Institute of Mechatronics Engineering, Tsinghua University. His research interests include robotics, robotic motion planning, and intelligent control.

## Research Interests

- Robotics and control
- Multi-robot safety motion planning
- Humanoid whole-body control
- Inferential statistics
